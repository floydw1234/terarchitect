# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Terarchitect to be a useful, serious, and welcoming project for people building AI-native software delivery workflows. Be respectful, constructive, and direct.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent, but communicate clearly.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, docs, and reproducible behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not share private credentials, logs, repositories, or customer data.
- Help keep discussions useful for maintainers and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, credential leaks, or unsafe instructions.
- Repeated low-effort noise after maintainers ask for focus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject contributions; or block participants who harm the project or community.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
